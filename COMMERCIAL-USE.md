# Commercial Use & Licensing

The AI Decision Governance Framework is a protected governance specification.

## Commercial and Institutional Use
Any use of this framework by companies, startups, governments, regulators, or institutions — including but not limited to:
- AI governance
- Compliance frameworks
- Risk management systems
- Audit and decision accountability
- Policy enforcement

requires a separate commercial license.

## Licensing Inquiries
For licensing, partnerships, or authorized use inquiries, contact the framework owner directly.

Unauthorized commercial or institutional use is prohibited.
