# Decision Authority Layer (DAL)
## A Governance Framework for Human‑Final Authority in AI Systems

Author: Mr Parivar  
Version: v1.0-draft  
Status: Public Governance Draft  
Timestamp: GitHub Commit

Abstract:
The Decision Authority Layer (DAL) defines a non‑delegable governance boundary where final decision authority remains explicitly human or legally institutional. This framework prevents autonomous AI systems from becoming de facto sovereign actors by enforcing a permanent human override layer.
